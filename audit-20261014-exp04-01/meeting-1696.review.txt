PILOT SMALL FACTS
1. Council Bill 1421 seeks to rezone a parcel at 14 2038 street from its current R.M. 30 with waivers zoning to CMC's eight.
2. The tiny home village currently operating on Walnut Street, just over the alley from the subject site, is seeking to relocate to the rezoned property due to an expiring six-month temporary use permit.

PILOT LARGE FACTS
1. Council Bill 1421 seeks to rezone a parcel at 38th and Blake from former Chapter 59 zoning to CMC’s Eight (urban center neighborhood context mixed use).
2. The property is currently vacant with some building footings remaining and is directly across the street from the 38th and Blake RTD Station.
3. The purpose of the rezoning is to allow the relocation of a Tiny Homes Village, which is operating under a six-month temporary unlisted use permit set to expire later this month.
4. Urban Land Conservancy owns the land and plans to develop 65 units of affordable housing on the site where the Tiny Homes Village is moving from, and 325 market-rate apartments on the Blake Street side in partnership with McWhinney.
5. Support for the rezoning was expressed by various community groups, including the Colorado Village Collaborative and the Black Star Action Movement, citing the success of the Tiny Homes Village model.
6. CPD and partner agencies are working on a broader project to examine zoning codes related to group living, including tiny homes, but no specific timeline has been announced.
7. The Tiny Homes Village currently provides 16 residents with safe housing at a cost of $5.80 per person per night and has had zero police calls during its 180-day operation.
8. There is concern about the non-renewable nature of the temporary use permit due to the lack of specific provisions in the zoning code accommodating tiny homes.
9. The city's TOD Fund was used to assist with the acquisition of the land, and there is an obligation to ensure affordability through that program.
10. Council members expressed support for the rezoning as a stopgap measure while advocating for a long-term solution for permanent tiny home communities in Denver.
